KOL IDS™ · CAMPAIGN LIFECYCLE + ROI CLARITY · 2026-10-05

1. Multiple campaigns per subscription
- A customer can create and retain multiple independent campaigns under the same active subscription.
- Campaign History remains the archive for all campaigns in the organization.
- Each campaign keeps its own downstream records and report context.

2. Campaign completion
- DRAFT = campaign is still being worked on.
- COMPLETE = Step 07 Campaign Intelligence Report has been reached successfully.
- Opening Step 07 marks the selected campaign COMPLETE automatically.
- Editing and saving a completed campaign returns it to DRAFT so the updated analysis must reach the report again.
- Campaign History uses distinct COMPLETE / DRAFT status treatments.

3. ROI / ROAS clarity
- ROAS is shown with: (Revenue ÷ Spend)
- ROI is shown with: ((Revenue − Spend) ÷ Spend × 100)
- Existing calculation logic is unchanged.
