# KOL IDS V30.2 — Smart Assist UX

## Goal
Make the existing 7-step KOL IDS workflow feel easier and faster without changing the existing analysis engine, decision logic, data IDs, or workflow structure.

## Added
- A lightweight **KOL IDS Smart Assist** panel at the top of each of the 7 steps.
- Guidance explains what the customer actually needs to provide and what KOL IDS does with that information.
- Beginner-friendly language: users can provide information in normal business language instead of knowing KOL/marketing terminology first.
- Explicitly reinforces the product principle: **Less input · Full intelligence**.

## Preserved
- All 7 steps remain available; no step locking was introduced.
- Existing Audience quick-brief and advanced intelligence structure remains intact.
- Existing Creator bio/profile inference and advanced manual signals remain intact.
- Existing Performance basic + advanced metrics remain intact.
- Existing analysis/decision engine is not replaced or rewritten by Smart Assist.
- Existing downstream payloads and derived calculations remain intact.

## Design principle
Customer effort should decrease while analytical depth remains available. Smart Assist is an interface/education layer around the existing intelligence system, not a new scoring engine.
